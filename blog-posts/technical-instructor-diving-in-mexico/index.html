Discover the Depths of Mexico: Technical Diving with Devocean Divers

Technical diving opens an entirely new world of underwater exploration. With over 70% of our planet covered by water, some of Earth’s greatest wonders lie beyond recreational limits — in deep oceans, submerged caverns, and ancient limestone tunnels.

In Mexico, those hidden frontiers are closer than you think. From Quintana Roo’s iconic cenotes and caves to deep Caribbean walls and wrecks off Cozumel, this country is a global hotspot for technical diving.

At Devocean Divers Mexico, we help divers and instructors push beyond limits — safely, professionally, and with passion.

The Rise of Technical Diving in Mexico

Mexico has become a global hub for PADI TecRec diving. Training is more accessible than ever, gear technology continues to evolve, and more divers are exploring advanced environments every year.

Whether you’re mastering decompression skills in Cenote El Pit, exploring Angelita’s hydrogen sulfide cloud, or preparing for deep wall dives near Cozumel, Devocean Divers Mexico provides the experience and mentorship to help you succeed.

Become a PADI Techical Instructor with Devocean Divers

The demand for skilled TecRec Instructors is growing across Mexico — and Devocean Divers offers a full pathway for divers ready to teach, guide, and inspire the next generation of technical explorers.

PADI TECHNICAL DIVING PATH

PADI Tec 40 Instructor & Tec 40 Trimix Instructor

The first step in the TecRec pathway. Learn to teach Tec Basics Diver, Tec 40 Diver, and Discover Tec experiences.

Introduces limited decompression dives within 40m.

Develops foundational gas management and buoyancy skills.

Trimix Instructors also teach safer deep dives using helium-based gas.

This is where many divers first fall in love with tech — and Mexico’s clear cenotes are the perfect classroom.

PADI Tec 45 Instructor & Tec 45 Trimix Instructor

Tec 45 is the transition from introduction to true technical diving. Students learn to plan and execute deeper, longer dives using double cylinders and unlimited decompression.

Tec 45 Trimix adds the ability to teach divers to manage helium gas blends for improved safety and performance.

At this level, instructors guide divers through the thrill of independence and precision — essential qualities in cave and deep diving.

PADI Tec 50 Instructor & Tec 50 Trimix Instructor

At the top of the open-circuit TecRec path, instructors at this level can train divers all the way to extended-range exploration.

The Tec 50 Trimix Instructor program qualifies you to teach divers how to safely explore advanced environments like:

Deep cenotes such as El Pit

The hydrogen cloud of Angelita

Offshore Caribbean walls near Playa del Carmen and Cozumel

A Tec 50 Instructor is a cornerstone of any professional dive team — especially in Mexico, where technical diving is thriving.
