Meet Walter Schmit.

A shared passion for diving. A very personal way of sharing it.

Your guide. Your instructor.
Your next conversation.

Devocean began in the Philippines in 2015, founded by Marcus Benders. Walter and Dorianne were part of the Malapascua team, and now lead Devocean Divers Mexico in Playa del Carmen.

They’ve built the operation around small groups, personal instruction and dive plans that reflect the individual. New to diving, coming back to the water or pursuing advanced training — it starts with a conversation.

Walter’s own diving story began in Puerto Madryn, Argentina in 2001. His path has taken him through recreational, professional, technical, cave and rebreather diving.
