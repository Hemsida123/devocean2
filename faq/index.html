A little preparation.
A better dive.

Answers to help you plan your time in Playa del Carmen.

Can I dive if I’ve never tried it before?

Yes — the Try-Dive experience starts with exercises in a pool or cenote and continues with an open-water dive. Tell us about your experience so we can recommend a suitable introduction.

Do I need certification for a cenote dive?

For guided cavern excursions, Devocean requires at least Open Water certification and a minimum age of 15. Bring your certification card. Cave diving beyond the light zone requires separate cave training and certification.

What’s included in the price?

Inclusions depend on the experience. Cenote entrance fees, camera fees and guide tips are additional. We will confirm equipment, course materials, certification costs and any other charges in your personal quote.

How do I make a booking?

Send your dates, the number of divers, your certification level and the experiences you’re interested in. We’ll confirm availability, meeting arrangements, the full price and the applicable deposit and cancellation terms before you commit.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, dry clothes, a water bottle, your certification and your own gear if you plan to use it. Let us know your equipment needs, sizes and whether your regulator uses a DIN or INT connection.

How big are the groups?

Cavern groups are limited to four divers per guide. Private guiding can also be arranged. Site selection is discussed with your guide and depends on experience, conditions and availability.

Where do we meet?

We coordinate a meeting point before your trip. For cenote trips, the published booking terms specify meeting points along the Playa del Carmen–Tulum route rather than hotel pickup. Confirm arrangements for your chosen activity.

What if the weather changes?

Ocean trips depend on harbour and sea conditions. A cenote alternative or a new date may be discussed if the harbour closes. Dive sites and schedules can change; ask which cancellation terms apply to your booking.

What medical paperwork is required?

Ask the team for the applicable medical questionnaire and liability documents before booking. If clearance is required, arrange it before your activity. Do not submit personal medical information through the enquiry form.

Can I bring a camera?

Tell us beforehand. Rules and permit charges differ between cenotes, and some sites restrict cameras. A professional photo session can also be arranged.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Contact us as soon as possible. The website and downloadable price list currently show different deposit and cancellation terms, so ask for the conditions that apply to your booking in writing before paying.

Will I definitely see sharks?

Wildlife encounters depend on natural conditions and are not guaranteed. Bull shark diving is advertised for November to March. Ask about current suitability and availability before planning your trip.
