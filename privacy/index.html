How this website handles contact and external services.

Contact information

When you submit the enquiry form, your name, email address, chosen experience, optional travel date and message are sent to Devocean Divers Mexico by email so the team can respond. The form does not save a separate database of enquiries. The email provider and website host process information needed to deliver the message.

Form protection

An essential session cookie protects form submissions and helps prevent duplicate sends. Hashed network identifiers, request counts and timestamps are temporarily stored for abuse prevention, without the contents of your enquiry.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Tripadvisor, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube open services operated by those providers. Their own privacy policies apply. Videos hosted on this website load when you choose to play them.

No payment or medical details

Do not include payment details or medical records in this form. Ask Devocean how to provide any required documents securely.

Questions about your message

Contact devoceandivers.mexico@gmail.com about information you have sent to Devocean.
