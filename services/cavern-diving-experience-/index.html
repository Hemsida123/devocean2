Cenote diving

Sunlit caverns. Crystal-clear water. A different side of Mexico.

A day beneath the jungle

Explore two cavern dives selected for your experience and comfort. Stay within the natural light zone and discover the remarkable formations of the Riviera Maya.

  • Minimum Open Water certification and age 15.
  • Maximum four divers per guide; private guiding can be arranged.
  • Two guided dives, with the site plan confirmed before departure.
  • Bring your certification, swimwear, a towel and dry clothes.
