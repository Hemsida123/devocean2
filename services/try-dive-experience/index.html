Your first dive

Start with the basics. Discover the feeling of breathing underwater.

Start at your own pace

You don’t need a certification to ask about a Try-Dive experience. Your instructor introduces the equipment and the essential skills in a pool or cenote before an open-water dive in a cenote or the ocean.

  1. Talk through the experience and complete the required paperwork.
  2. Practise basic skills with your instructor in a training environment.
  3. Take those first underwater breaths into an open-water experience.

Ready to learn more?

The PADI Open Water course combines knowledge development, confined-water practice and four open-water dives. Ask which path fits your travel plans.
